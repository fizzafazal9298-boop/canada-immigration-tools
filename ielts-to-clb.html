IELTS to CLB converter

Canada scores language in Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB). Your IELTS bands are converted ability by ability — and because CRS points also work ability by ability, one weak skill can cost you twice.

Reading is the strictest scale: a 6.0 in reading is CLB 7, while a 6.0 in listening is also CLB 7 — but writing 6.0 is only CLB 7 too, and the jump to CLB 9 needs 7.0 in reading, writing and speaking, and 8.0 in listening.

Other tests

  • CELPIP-General: the score for each ability is the CLB level (a CELPIP 9 = CLB 9). Scores below 4 sit under CLB 4.
  • PTE Core: accepted for Express Entry, with its own equivalency chart published by IRCC — check your exact scores against the chart on canada.ca.
  • French: TEF Canada and TCF Canada results convert to NCLC levels, the French equivalent of CLB. NCLC 7 in all four abilities unlocks the French additional points.
CLB 9 in all four abilities is the magic line for most candidates: it maximises the education × language transferability tier. Once you know your CLB levels, put them into the CRS calculator.
