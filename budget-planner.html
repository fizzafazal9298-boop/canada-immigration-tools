Three budgeting truths

  • Proof of funds is not a fee — it stays your money, but you must show it, untouched, when you apply and when you land.
  • Language tests are a recurring cost until you hit your target CLB. Budget the retake now, not after the disappointment.
  • The settlement buffer decides your first year. Candidates who land with three calm months of money make better job decisions than candidates who must take the first offer.
